Helmsman DSX1 uses the native Superb Warfare AWM gameplay implementation.
The DSX1 animation resource and empty bone scaffold are adapted from the AWM
resources in Superb Warfare 0.8.9.2, commit 65d985a64 (Minecraft 1.20.1).
Upstream: https://github.com/Mercurows/SuperbWarfare
Upstream authors: Atsuishio, Roki27, Light_Quanta and other contributors.
Upstream mod license: GNU General Public License v3.0.
Changes: removed native cubes and absent animation targets; adjusted DSX1
bolt/magazine pivots and left hand anchor; added own mesh binding leaves;
reduced handle lift to 30 degrees and retargeted the matching right hand keys
to clear the DSX1 scope housing while retaining native timing and bolt travel.
The DSX1 mesh, UV atlas and material resources are the project's authored assets.
Sounds and crosshair are referenced from installed SBW; no duplicate sound files.
