The default hexBot(4, 1.0) FLAT torso and BASE/FEMUR/TIBIA/TIP block-model
declarations are translated to the adjacent hexbot.json, retaining source matrices,
vanilla block states, cloak material substitutions and the cyan eye.
Minecraft block geometry and textures are provided by Minecraft at runtime.
DNV's player vehicle, server motion, foot snapshots and client rendering are adaptations.
