# DNV 1.20 · Roar of the Behemoths

**Minecraft 1.20.1 / Forge · Minecraft 1.21.1 / NeoForge**

## What's new since 1.1.76

- **Libertad battleship.** Take the helm of a 32-knot battleship. Fire the main battery, enable automatic secondaries and let four anti-aircraft sectors engage incoming targets. In Mode 2, crew members can operate the guns on their side. To film from the ship in Mode 1, take a side observation seat and use F6 to set up a tracking shot.
- **Yue-class aircraft carrier.** Stock three hangar bays, bring a fighter up on the lift and launch it from the bow. Up to two fighters can operate at once. A returning pilot can request recovery, land under carrier guidance and stow the aircraft in its original bay. Run the deck solo or with a crew. At the helm, set a waypoint on the tactical map and choose cruise or warp.
- **Feiying carrier fighter.** Switch between jet and hover flight. The 30 mm cannon follows the nose in jet mode and can traverse in hover. Four underwing missiles share slot 2, can lock visible hostile air or ground targets at any altitude, and use SBW large anti-ground missiles for resupply. Switching back to the cannon retains the missiles on the wings.
- **Survival shipbuilding.** Research and copy the Libertad and Yue blueprints at the SBW research station, then assemble the ships from sections at the vehicle assembling table. A newly crafted Yue starts with three empty bays. Build each Feiying separately, then put its crate into a bay or deploy it on its own.
- **In-game player guides.** Pick up either language edition in the tools section of the DNV creative tab. Both books have a clickable table of contents and 29 chapters covering ships, flight operations, artillery, vehicles, ammunition and power. Pick up a fresh copy for the latest instructions.

## Gameplay changes

- Once everyone has disembarked, sneak-use a crowbar to pack a Yue even while it hovers. At the new location, place the crate and open it with the crowbar. Previously packed hovering carriers and independently deployed Feiyings in either flight mode can also be unpacked at the new site.
- Blizzard’s second mode now fires a charged beam. Land on solid ground, wait for the cover to open, then hold fire for three seconds. The beam pierces entities up to 80 blocks away, stops at terrain and takes two seconds to cool down. Releasing early lets the charge fade gradually.
- Twin Bofors, Quad Bofors and Flak38 deal more damage with both AP and HE. Their AP rounds leave terrain intact. The nuclear silo’s blast now clears terrain and liquids outward from the center, with smoke and fire following its progress.

# DNV 1.20 · 巨兽轰鸣

**Minecraft 1.20.1 / Forge · Minecraft 1.21.1 / NeoForge**

## 自 1.1.76 以来的新增玩法

- **自由号战列舰。** 平水满速 32 节。主驾驶可操纵主炮、开启自动副炮，舰上的四个防空区可分别迎击来敌。切到模式二，两侧炮手可以各自开火。想拍摄航行画面，可在模式一坐进任一侧观战席，用 F6 调整并锁定跟拍镜头。
- **岳级航空母舰。** 三个机库仓位可存放飞鹰。战机由升降台送上甲板，从舰艏弹射，最多同时派出两架。飞行员请求回收后，母舰引导战机着舰，停稳后可收入原仓位。单人可完成出动与回收，也可与舰长分工。舰长可在战术地图设定航点，选择巡航或跃迁。
- **飞鹰型舰载机。** 可切换固定翼和悬停姿态。固定翼的 30 毫米机炮随机头瞄准，悬停时炮座可以转动。四枚翼下导弹共用第 2 武器槽，可在任意高度锁定视线内的敌方空中或地面目标，使用卓越前线的大型对地导弹补给。切回机炮会保留翼下挂弹。
- **生存造舰。** 在卓越前线研究台研究或复制自由号、岳级蓝图，再到载具装配台用分段组件总装。生存造出的岳级三个机库仓位起初都为空。飞鹰单独制作，装箱后既可放入机库，也可独立部署。
- **游戏内玩法手册。** DNV 创造栏的工具区可领取中文或英文成书。两本手册各有 29 章，点击目录就能查到舰船驾驶、甲板起降、火炮、载具、弹药及供电。背包里已有旧书的玩家可重新领取新版。

## 玩法调整

- 岳级悬停时，全员下舰后即可潜行用撬棍收箱。在新地点放下箱子，再用撬棍开箱即可放出。旧版收起的悬停岳级，以及独立部署后收起的固定翼或悬停飞鹰，也能在新位置放出。
- 暴风雪二模式改为蓄力光束。落到固体地面、等舱盖打开后，按住开火 3 秒。光束最远贯穿 80 格内的实体，遇地形阻挡停止，发射后冷却 2 秒。提前松键时，蓄力会逐渐消退。
- 双联博福斯、四联博福斯和 Flak38 的穿甲弹与高爆弹伤害提高，三款轻炮的穿甲弹保留地形。核弹发射井的爆炸会从中心向外逐步清除地形和液体，烟柱与余火随之展开。
