# DNV 1.1.76

**Minecraft 1.20.1 / Forge · Minecraft 1.21.1 / NeoForge**

## Content highlights

- **Six artillery guns:** Bofors Twin, Bofors Quad, Flak38, Flak88, Flak40, and M1 Heavy Cannon. Operate the three lighter guns from their seats, or control the three heavy guns through a terminal.
- **Helmsman DSX1:** a bolt-action sniper rifle with a five-round magazine, 4–8× scope, fluorescent green reticle, and warm white and gold tracer.
- **HexBot:** a six-legged vehicle with moving legs and a body that follows the terrain.

## Improvements

- Improved artillery aiming views and crosshair display.
- Updated DSX1 aiming and arm poses, scope reticle, and illuminated details.
- Added ammunition and loading guidance for the three remote heavy guns.
- Extended Blizzard's first-mode energy pulse range to 256 blocks and added fast automatic energy recharge.

## Known issue

Blizzard's second mode currently has a bug. A fix is in progress.

## Requirements

- **Minecraft 1.20.1 / Forge:** Superb Warfare 0.8.9.1 or 0.8.9.2.
- **Minecraft 1.21.1 / NeoForge:** Superb Warfare 0.8.9.1, 0.8.9.1-hotfix, 0.8.9.1-final, or 0.8.9.2.

# 中文更新日志

## 本版内容亮点

- **六款火炮：**双联博福斯、四联博福斯、Flak38、Flak88、Flak40 和 M1 重型火炮。三款轻炮可进入炮位操作，三款重炮可通过操作台遥控。
- **舵手 DSX1：**配备 5 发弹匣、4–8 倍瞄准镜、荧光绿分划和暖白淡金色弹道拖尾的栓动狙击步枪。
- **HexBot 六足载具：**驾驶六足机械载具，体验机械腿与机身随地形调整的动作。

## 调整与优化

- 优化部分火炮的瞄准视角与准星显示。
- 调整舵手的开镜与手部姿态，更新镜内分划和发光细节。
- 为三座遥控重炮增加弹药类型和装填方式提示。
- 暴风雪一阶段能量弹射程提升至 256 格，并加入快速自动能量装填。

## 已知问题

暴风雪第二阶段目前有 BUG，正在修复中。

- **Minecraft 1.21.1 / NeoForge：**卓越前线 0.8.9.1、0.8.9.1-hotfix、0.8.9.1-final 或 0.8.9.2。
