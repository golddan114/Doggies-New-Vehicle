# Doggie's New Vehicle / 狗狗的新车

## English

**Roar of the Behemoths**

Doggie's New Vehicle is an unofficial add-on for [Superb Warfare](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/superb-warfare), expanding its battlefield with automated defenses, heavy artillery, armored vehicles, hover tanks, allied AI support, and large combat units.

Build your defenses, take control of powerful vehicles, or bring strategic weapons into your world.

### Take command at sea and in the air

- **Libertad battleship.** Reach 32 knots on calm water. At the helm, fire the main battery and switch on automatic secondaries. Four anti-aircraft sectors cover the ship. In Mode 1, use either side observation seat to frame and lock a tracking shot.
- **Yue-class aircraft carrier.** Set a waypoint on the tactical map to cruise or warp. Research the blueprint, assemble the hull at an SBW vehicle assembling table and stock the three empty hangar bays with Feiying crates. Lock the deck, take the lift up and launch. Up to two fighters can be out at once. Once everyone disembarks, sneak-use a crowbar to pack the carrier even while it hovers. Place the crate at your destination and open it with the crowbar.
- **Feiying carrier fighter.** Fly in jet mode or switch to hover. Slot 1 fires the 30 mm cannon. Slot 2 carries four missiles that lock visible air or ground targets at any altitude. Request recovery and return to your original hangar bay, or deploy the fighter on its own.

Pick up the English or Simplified Chinese **DNV Player Guide** from the tools section of the DNV creative tab. Use the contents to jump to shipbuilding, deck operations, vehicle controls or troubleshooting.

### Main Features

- **Blizzard:** A player-controlled hover tank equipped with a cryogenic pulse cannon, a grounded laser mode, and TV-guided cryogenic missiles. Hover over terrain and fluid surfaces, perform a short vertical boost, or deploy into a stationary firing position.
- **Nuclear Silo:** A deployable launch facility with animated loading and launch sequences, coordinate-targeted nuclear missiles, radiation effects, and survival crafting. A remote deployment device lets you unfold the silo from a safe distance.
- **Dog Egg Silo:** Launch the Vacuum Dog Egg, a special payload that pulls nearby enemies toward its impact area before releasing an implosion effect.
- **Automated Defense Turrets:** The Type 50 Changfeng Heavy Cannon, Storm Twin Autocannon Turret, and Jingzhe Thunder Tower provide different FE-powered defensive roles.
- **Six Artillery Guns:** Twin and quad Bofors, Flak38, Flak88, Flak40, and the M1 Heavy Cannon. Choose between operating a gun from its seat and controlling heavy artillery through a remote console.
- **Helmsman DSX1:** A bolt-action sniper rifle with a five-round magazine, a 4–8× scope, a fluorescent green reticle, and a warm white and gold tracer.
- **HexBot Six-legged Walker:** A drivable six-legged vehicle with moving legs and a body that adjusts to the terrain.
- **Apocalypse Heavy Tank:** A drivable armored vehicle with dual main guns, selectable ammunition, rockets, and guided missiles.
- **Project 612:** An armored vehicle with a powerful gun in a fixed fighting compartment. Turn the hull to line up your shots.
- **Allied AI Apocalypse:** An independent AI variant that guards its deployment area and engages hostile mobs and bosses.
- **Large Combat Units:** Encounter Hover Cube Boss (Prototype), Noise, Silent Heaven, and the SES Executor of Truth, each with its own arsenal and combat behavior.
- **Survival Progression:** Blueprint research and copying, vehicle assembly recipes, custom missile components, and FE-powered equipment.
- **English and Simplified Chinese:** Both languages are included. Names and messages follow your Minecraft language setting.

### Six Artillery Guns

Build an anti-aircraft position, defend an approach, or set up a heavy artillery battery. Each gun has its own model, moving barrel assembly, and firing recoil.

| Gun | Armament | Operation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bofors Twin | Two 40 mm barrels | Operate from the gun seat |
| Bofors Quad | Four 40 mm barrels | Operate from the gun seat |
| Flak38 | Four 20 mm barrels | Operate from the gun seat |
| Flak88 | 88 mm gun | Remote console |
| Flak40 | Twin 128 mm heavy AA gun | Remote console |
| M1 Heavy Cannon | Heavy artillery | Remote console; also supports the artillery indicator |

**Bofors Twin, Bofors Quad, and Flak38** support AP and HE ammunition. Load the gun and take a seat to start firing; these three guns do not require FE power.

**Flak88, Flak40, and M1** use remote consoles. Bind a console to your gun, load its ammunition, activate it, and provide FE power. You can then take control from the placed console while your character stays beside it. Press **F5** to switch camera views.

The console's messages tell you which ammunition to use. To load a gun, right-click the gun itself with an empty hand to open its inventory. M1 can also receive coordinate-targeted fire commands through Superb Warfare's artillery indicator.

All six guns have blueprint recipes at Superb Warfare's vehicle assembling table. To try them right away, find their deployment items in the DNV creative inventory tab.

### Helmsman DSX1 Sniper Rifle

Take the Helmsman DSX1 to your next firing position. It uses **Superb Warfare sniper ammunition**, holds **five rounds**, and comes with a **4–8× scope**.

- A fluorescent green reticle with a dark outline makes aiming easier against bright backgrounds such as snow.
- The bolt, charging handle, and magazine move during firing, cycling, and reloading.
- A warm white and gold tracer follows your shots.
- Illuminated details give the rifle a distinctive appearance.

### Blizzard Controls

- **G:** Switch vehicle mode. This key can be reassigned in Controls.
- **V:** Activate the vertical boost while in hover mode.
- In Mode 2, land on solid ground. Hold fire for three seconds to release a piercing beam, then allow two seconds for cooldown.
- **TV missile guidance:** Fire the missile, steer with the mouse, and press **Esc** to leave the missile view.

### Installation

Superb Warfare is required. Install the matching Minecraft and loader versions of Superb Warfare and its required dependencies.

| Minecraft | Mod Loader | Java | Superb Warfare |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1.20.1 | Forge 47.4.18+ (47.x) | Java 17 | 0.8.9.2, build `65d985a64` |
| 1.21.1 | NeoForge 21.1.227+ (21.1.x) | Java 21 | 0.8.9.2 hotfix, build `e0c2b27f` |

For the Forge build above, use GeckoLib 4.8.4.

Libertad and Yue naval aviation are included in the DNV file. Remove their older standalone builds when upgrading.

Install the corresponding DNV version on both the client and server. When updating, replace the older DNV JAR.

Forge and NeoForge use separate files. DNV builds listed here require Superb Warfare 0.8.9.2.

## 中文

**巨兽轰鸣**

狗狗的新车是为[《卓越前线》Superb Warfare](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/superb-warfare)制作的非官方附属模组，加入自动防御炮塔、重型火炮、装甲载具、悬浮坦克、友方 AI 支援和大型战斗单位。

你可以建设防御阵地，亲自驾驶载具，也可以部署战略武器，扩展自己的战场。

### 驾驶战舰与舰载机

- **自由号战列舰。** 平水满速 32 节。主驾驶操纵主炮，也能开启自动副炮，四个防空区负责近舰防空。在模式一的任一侧观战席可以调整并锁定跟拍镜头。
- **岳级航空母舰。** 舰长可在战术地图设定航点，选择巡航或跃迁。研究蓝图，在卓越前线的载具装配台总装舰体。生存造出的新舰有三个空仓，装入飞鹰集装箱后即可锁定甲板、乘升降台登机并弹射，最多同时派出两架。全员下舰后，悬停中的岳级也能潜行用撬棍收箱。在新地点放下箱子，用撬棍开箱即可放出。
- **飞鹰型舰载机。** 可在固定翼与悬停间切换。第 1 槽是 30 毫米机炮，第 2 槽的四枚导弹可在任意高度锁定视线内的敌方空中或地面目标。飞鹰可以独立部署，也能请求回舰、停稳后收入原仓位。

在 DNV 创造栏的工具区可领取中文或英文《DNV 游玩指南》。点击目录可直达造舰、甲板操作、载具驾驶和故障排查等章节。

### 主要内容

- **暴风雪：** 玩家可驾驶的悬浮坦克，配备冰冻粒子主炮、接地后的激光模式，以及 TV 遥控冰冻导弹。支持地形与液面悬浮、短时跃升和固定阵地射击。
- **核弹发射井：** 可部署的战略发射设施，拥有完整装填与发射动画、指定坐标打击、核爆及辐射效果，并提供生存合成。远程部署器允许玩家退离井口后展开设施。
- **狗蛋发射井：** 可发射“真空大狗蛋”，将附近敌人吸向落点区域，再释放内爆效果。
- **自动防御炮塔：** 50式“长风”重型火炮、“暴雨”自动炮塔和“惊蛰”型五雷天师塔，使用 FE 能源，承担不同的防御任务。
- **六款火炮：** 双联博福斯、四联博福斯、Flak38、Flak88、Flak40 和 M1 重型火炮，提供进入炮位操作与操作台遥控两种体验。
- **舵手 DSX1：** 配备 5 发弹匣、4–8 倍瞄准镜、荧光绿分划和暖白淡金色弹道拖尾的栓动狙击步枪。
- **HexBot 六足载具：** 玩家可驾驶的六足机械载具，机械腿与机身会随地形调整姿态。
- **天启重型坦克：** 玩家可驾驶，配备双主炮、可切换弹种、火箭与制导导弹。
- **工程612：** 采用固定战斗室与大威力主炮的装甲载具，通过转动车体对准目标。
- **友方 AI 天启：** 独立的 AI 版本，警戒部署区域，自动打击敌对怪物与 BOSS。
- **大型战斗单位：** 悬浮 CUBE BOSS（原型）、噪声、死寂天堂和 SES 真理执行者，各有独立的武器与战斗行为。
- **生存发展：** 包含蓝图研究与复制、载具装配配方、自定义导弹组件及 FE 能源装备。
- **内置中英双语：** 简体中文与英文均已包含，名称和提示随 Minecraft 的语言设置切换。

### 六款火炮

架设防空阵地、封锁进攻路线，或部署一组遥控重炮。每款火炮都有独立外观、炮管运动与开火后坐表现。

| 火炮 | 武器配置 | 操作方式 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 双联博福斯 | 双联 40 毫米炮 | 进入炮位操作 |
| 四联博福斯 | 四联 40 毫米炮 | 进入炮位操作 |
| Flak38 | 四联 20 毫米炮 | 进入炮位操作 |
| Flak88 | 88 毫米炮 | 操作台遥控 |
| Flak40 | 双联 128 毫米重型高射炮 | 操作台遥控 |
| M1 重型火炮 | 重型火力支援 | 操作台遥控，也可使用火炮指示器 |

**双联博福斯、四联博福斯和 Flak38** 支持 AP／HE 弹药切换，装好弹药后即可进入炮位开火，三款轻炮无需 FE 供电。

**Flak88、Flak40 和 M1** 通过操作台遥控。绑定操作台，为炮塔装弹、激活并提供 FE 供电后，就能在放置好的操作台接管火炮。玩家身体留在操作台旁，按 **F5** 可以切换观察视角。

操作台提示会说明需要的弹药。装填时，空手右键炮塔本体即可打开库存。M1 还可以配合卓越前线的火炮指示器，执行指定坐标射击。

六款火炮均可使用对应蓝图，在卓越前线的载具装配台中制作。想快速体验，也可以在创造模式的 DNV 物品栏中找到对应部署物品。

### 舵手 DSX1 狙击步枪

带上舵手 DSX1，寻找你的下一个狙击位置。这把栓动狙击步枪使用**卓越前线狙击步枪弹药**，拥有 **5 发弹匣**和 **4–8 倍瞄准镜**。

- **荧光绿分划与深色描边：** 在雪地等明亮背景下也便于辨认。
- **枪械动作：** 枪栓、拉柄与弹匣会随射击、拉栓和换弹动作运动。
- **弹道拖尾：** 开火后留下暖白与淡金色轨迹。
- **发光细节：** 枪身带有独立的发光装饰。

### 暴风雪快捷操作

- **G：** 切换载具模式，可在控制设置中改绑。
- **V：** 在悬浮模式下进行短时跃升。
- 二模式落在固体地面后，按住开火蓄力 3 秒发射贯穿光束，每次发射后冷却 2 秒。
- **TV 导弹遥控：** 发射后用鼠标控制方向，按 **Esc** 退出导弹视角。
