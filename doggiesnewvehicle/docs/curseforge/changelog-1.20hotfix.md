# DNV 1.20hotfix

**Minecraft 1.20.1 / Forge · Minecraft 1.21.1 / NeoForge**

## Fixes

- **Yue carrier.** If a Feiying is destroyed before taking off, its deck slot and hangar bay are freed so you can prepare a replacement without redeploying the carrier. Hovering carriers can still be packed and redeployed with the crowbar.
- **Allied AI Apocalypse.** Smoothed tank movement and turret turning. The main gun, rockets and missiles now wait until the turret is aligned. Removed the overlapping faces in the side skirts on both sides of the Apocalypse.
- **Clearer feedback in English and Chinese.** The Yue explains why its deck will not lock and reports obstruction coordinates where available. Libertad reports helm, mode, cooldown and close-in defense power requirements. Silo messages identify target, ownership, preparation, energy and launch clearance problems.
- **Player guides.** Fresh English and Chinese copies in the DNV creative tab cover the revised deck, fire control and silo instructions.

## Requirements

| Minecraft | Loader | Java | Superb Warfare |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1.20.1 | Forge 47.4.18+ (47.x) | 17 | 0.8.9.2, build `65d985a64` |
| 1.21.1 | NeoForge 21.1.227+ (21.1.x) | 21 | 0.8.9.2 hotfix, build `e0c2b27f` |

Use matching DNV and Superb Warfare builds on both client and server. Use GeckoLib 4.8.4 on Forge. Replace the old DNV JAR and remove any standalone Libertad or Yue trial JARs.

# DNV 1.20hotfix 中文更新日志

**Minecraft 1.20.1 / Forge · Minecraft 1.21.1 / NeoForge**

## 修复与调整

- **岳级航空母舰。** 飞鹰在甲板上被击毁后会释放甲板与对应仓格，可以重新装入一架飞鹰准备出动。岳级悬停时仍可用撬棍收箱，再搬到新位置放出。
- **友军 AI 天启。** 改善车体移动和炮塔转向的连贯性。主炮、火箭和导弹会等炮塔瞄准到位再发射。修复天启两侧裙甲凹口的重叠面。
- **中英双语提示。** 岳级会说明甲板无法固定的原因，能定位障碍时会报出坐标。自由号会提示舰桥权限、模式、冷却和近防供电。发射井会说明目标、所有者权限、准备进度、电量和发射净空问题。
- **游戏内玩法手册。** DNV 创造栏可重新领取中文或英文指南，查阅更新后的甲板、火控和发射井操作。
